_D_e_s_c_r_i_p_t_i_o_n:

     Results from 19 studies assessing the prevalence of depression
     after myocardial infarction

_U_s_a_g_e:

     dat.feng2019
     
_F_o_r_m_a_t:

     The data frame contains the following columns:

       *author*         'character'  first author                    
       *year*           'integer'    publication year                
       *region*         'character'  country                         
       *design*         'character'  study design                    
       *source*         'character'  sample source                   
       *age*            'numeric'    mean age                        
       *males*          'numeric'    percentage of males             
       *first*          'numeric'    percentage of first-time MI     
       *questionnaire*  'character'  self-report questionnaire       
       *interview*      'character'  structured interview            
       *timing*         'character'  timing of depression assessment 
       *depr*           'integer'    subjects with depression        
       *n*              'integer'    sample size                     
      
_D_e_t_a_i_l_s:

     This data set comes from a meta-analysis with 19 studies to
     estimate the prevalence of depression after a myocardial
     infarction. The variables 'depr' and 'n' contain the number of
     depressive patients and the total number of patients.

_C_o_n_c_e_p_t_s:

     psychology, cardiology, prevalence

     Feng, L., Li, L., Liu, W., Yang, J., Wang, Q., Shi, L., & Luo, M.
     (2019). Prevalence of depression in myocardial infarction: A
     PRISMA-compliant meta-analysis. _Medicine_, *98*(8), e14596.
