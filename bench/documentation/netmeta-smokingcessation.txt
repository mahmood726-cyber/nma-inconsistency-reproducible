_D_e_s_c_r_i_p_t_i_o_n:

     Network meta-analysis comparing the effects of a number of
     interventions for smoking cessation.

     These data are used as an example in Dias et al. (2013), page 651.

_F_o_r_m_a_t:

     A data frame with the following columns:

       *_event1_*  number of individuals with successful 
                   smoking cessation in arm 1            
           *_n1_*  number of individuals in arm 1        
       *_event2_*  number of individuals with successful 
                   smoking cessation in arm 2            
           *_n2_*  number of individuals in arm 2        
       *_event3_*  number of individuals with successful 
                   smoking cessation in arm 3            
           *_n3_*  number of individuals in arm 3        
       *_treat1_*  treatment 1                           
       *_treat2_*  treatment 2                           
       *_treat3_*  treatment 3                           
      
_S_o_u_r_c_e:

     Dias S, Welton NJ, Sutton AJ, Caldwell DM, Lu G and Ades AE
     (2013): Evidence Synthesis for Decision Making 4: Inconsistency in
     networks of evidence based on randomized controlled trials.
     _Medical Decision Making_, *33*, 641-56

     'pairwise', 'metabin', 'netmeta', 'netgraph.netmeta'
